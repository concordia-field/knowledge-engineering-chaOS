
## What
What this state holds, in one sentence.

## Why
Why it matters, and what depends on it.

## Who
Who sets it, who reads it, and who may change it.

## Where
Where it lives or applies: project, session, or file.

## When
When it changes, and when it resets.

## How
How it is derived from events. Cite the event ids.

## How much
Its scale: limits, thresholds, cost. Write `unknown` rather than guess.
