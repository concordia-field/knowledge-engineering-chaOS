# Notice

chaOS knowledge is built on the OSCARC method and on two works from the Fountain of Love operating model
(https://github.com/fountain-of-love/operating-model):

- **chaOS seed**: the spiral, its records (event, state, relation) and its hook, used unchanged.
- **OSCARC**: the six-step finding method, adapted into English from a Dutch source supplied by the maintainer.
- **knowledge-engineering**: the knowledge node, status and maturity, absence values, sources as nodes, audits and authority.
